# Observer-Certification and Copyright Boundary

Teaching Practice Lab is designed to help a teacher understand instructional reasoning and prepare for evidence-based conversations. It is not an observer-certification program and must not present itself as one.

## What current public MEC material establishes

Current Marzano Evaluation Center sources publicly establish that:

- FTEM observer certification/recertification exists,
- observation accuracy depends on shared criteria and evidence,
- implementation includes scoring consistency, calibration, and inter-rater agreement,
- iObservation contains the electronic Focused Teacher Evaluation Model Observation and Feedback Protocol,
- MEC professional development supports observer skill, feedback, calibration, and reliability.

## What Teaching Practice Lab may teach

Teaching Practice Lab may teach from publicly documented concepts such as:

- identifying instructional intent,
- recognizing the likely competency in a classroom case,
- connecting a strategy to a desired effect,
- distinguishing teacher moves from student evidence,
- identifying when evidence is incomplete,
- analyzing adaptations,
- practicing evidence-based post-observation reflection,
- comparing independent interpretations of a case,
- explaining why two reasonable observers might disagree.

## What Teaching Practice Lab must not claim

Do not claim that the platform:

- certifies an official Marzano observer,
- reproduces observer-certification training,
- grants district evaluator authority,
- produces an official FTEM observation score,
- guarantees inter-rater reliability,
- substitutes for district policy or contractual evaluation procedures,
- contains the complete licensed Observation and Feedback Protocol.

## Copyright boundary

Current MEC material states that iObservation provides electronic access to the FTEM Observation and Feedback Protocol. That protocol, full rubrics, detailed developmental scales, proprietary evidence descriptors, training videos, scoring exercises, and certification materials should not be copied into this project unless redistribution rights are established.

Teaching Practice Lab should instead create original:

- classroom cases,
- evidence sets,
- comparison exercises,
- reflective prompts,
- defense questions,
- calibration-style discussion exercises,
- diagrams explaining the publicly documented reasoning model.

## Wording rule

Prefer:

> "Teaching Practice Lab uses current public FTEM concepts to practice instructional reasoning."

Avoid:

> "This course trains you to score FTEM observations."

Prefer:

> "Compare the evidence each observer used and explain the disagreement."

Avoid:

> "Complete this official calibration test."

## District implementation boundary

Actual evaluation procedures may be shaped by:

- state requirements,
- district-adopted instruments,
- collective bargaining agreements,
- local observation schedules,
- district evaluator training,
- local scoring and conference procedures.

Those requirements must be researched separately before Teaching Practice Lab makes district-specific claims.
