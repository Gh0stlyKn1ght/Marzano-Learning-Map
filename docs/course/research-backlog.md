# Research Backlog

This file tracks what must be verified before Teaching Practice Lab turns a topic into published course material.

The rule is simple:

> If the source is incomplete, the course should say so. Do not fill framework gaps from memory.

## Priority 0 — Required before full FTEM authoring

### Standards-Based Planning exact labels

Current public Marzano Evaluation Center material clearly identifies a three-competency Planning domain and explicitly names **Planning Standards-Based Lessons/Units**, while describing the other planning areas.

Need:

- exact current canonical names of all three planning competencies,
- current desired-effect wording or sufficiently authoritative descriptions for internal analysis,
- current public evidence/technique descriptions that can be safely paraphrased,
- confirmation of any recent terminology changes.

Preferred source:

1. current official FTEM protocol/report,
2. current licensed district/state copy if legally available,
3. direct Marzano Evaluation Center material.

Do not encode guessed labels.

### Current model version history

Need a clean timeline documenting:

- 2011 Art and Science / 60-element Learning Map,
- development/release of the Focused Teacher Evaluation Model,
- significant public revisions or updates,
- whether any newer teacher model has formally superseded FTEM.

This timeline should distinguish **model release**, **document update**, and **website update**.

## Priority 1 — Course-depth research

### Identifying Critical Content

Collect current official material addressing:

- purpose,
- desired student effect,
- examples/techniques,
- evidence,
- common implementation errors.

Course questions:

- How is critical content different from writing an objective on the board?
- How should critical content be signaled in a CS lesson?
- What evidence indicates students know what is important rather than merely what was highlighted?

### Previewing New Content

Research:

- prior knowledge activation,
- relationship to upcoming content,
- evidence of connection,
- distinction from Reviewing Content.

### Processing New Content

Research:

- what counts as active processing,
- student evidence,
- role of chunking/interaction if current source supports it,
- distinction from elaboration.

### Elaborating on Content

Research:

- purpose of elaborative questioning,
- student explanation/inference,
- distinction from generic teacher questioning.

### Reviewing Content

Research:

- purpose/timing,
- evidence,
- distinction from previewing and retrieval practice as a learning-science technique.

Important: do not collapse Marzano's element into the broader cognitive-science concept of retrieval practice. They may overlap in implementation, but they are not automatically identical constructs.

### Practice Skills, Strategies, and Processes

Research:

- practice progression,
- feedback/error correction,
- procedural fluency,
- evidence of improvement.

### Similarities and Differences

Research:

- comparison/classification/analogy if supported by current material,
- desired effect,
- false positives such as simple feature listing.

### Examine Reasoning

Research:

- types of reasoning the current model emphasizes,
- student evidence,
- distinction between checking correctness and analyzing reasoning.

### Revise Knowledge

Research:

- revision of understanding/mental models,
- evidence of actual revision,
- distinction from merely correcting an answer.

### Cognitively Complex Tasks

Research:

- current definition/characteristics,
- relationship to knowledge utilization,
- distinction between complexity, difficulty, workload, and project length.

## Priority 2 — Conditions for Learning depth

### Formative Assessment vs Feedback

Need a precise sourced distinction between:

- gathering/tracking evidence of progress,
- providing feedback and recognizing progress.

### Interaction vs Engagement

Need current source material allowing us to explain why:

- group seating is not automatically interaction with content,
- visible enthusiasm is not automatically the desired effect of engagement strategies.

### Rules and Procedures

Need depth beyond compliance:

- how procedures support access to learning,
- appropriate evidence,
- teacher response when procedures break down.

### Relationships

Need current source framing for student-centered classroom relationships without drifting into unsourced generic relationship advice.

### High Expectations / Achievement Gap

Need careful authoritative sourcing because this topic can be flattened into slogans.

Research:

- what current FTEM materials mean by high expectations,
- evidence,
- equitable access/support,
- false positives such as identical treatment regardless of need.

## Priority 3 — Professional Responsibilities depth

### Policies and Procedures

Need scenarios where professional judgment and institutional requirements interact.

### Expertise in Content and Pedagogy

Research deliberate practice, professional learning, self-assessment, and maintaining expertise.

### Teacher Leadership and Collaboration

Research current Marzano framing and examples that distinguish productive collaboration from participation alone.

## Priority 4 — Observation and defense research

Need authoritative current material on:

- observation process,
- desired-effect monitoring,
- adaptation,
- student evidence,
- post-observation conversation,
- observer calibration/inter-rater reliability,
- teacher self-assessment.

Boundary:

Teaching Practice Lab can train reasoning from publicly documented concepts, but must not claim to provide official observer certification or reproduce licensed scoring protocols.

## Priority 5 — Learning science

Current foundation:

- retrieval practice,
- transfer through testing,
- distributed practice.

Potential additions to research carefully:

- self-explanation,
- worked examples and fading,
- interleaving/discrimination learning,
- feedback timing,
- desirable difficulties,
- generation effect.

Only add these when a primary or high-quality research source has been reviewed and the design implication is clear.

## Priority 6 — New Jersey implementation context

Research separately from FTEM itself:

- current NJDOE approved teacher-practice instrument rules,
- district flexibility,
- Middle Township's actual chosen instrument/version if a public source is available,
- district observation procedures if public,
- distinction between state requirements, district implementation, and Marzano model guidance.

Do not present district-specific claims until verified.

## Source note template

For every significant research addition, record:

```text
Claim:
Source:
Publisher:
Publication/update date:
Access date:
Primary or secondary:
What the source supports:
What it does NOT support:
Copyright/redistribution notes:
Course pages affected:
Open questions:
```

This makes later agent authoring much safer because the source boundary is explicit before content generation begins.
