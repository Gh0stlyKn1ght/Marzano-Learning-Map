# Professional Responsibilities Research Dossiers

These dossiers prepare the source-grounded knowledge needed to author the Professional Responsibilities course. They are **research records, not official Marzano protocols**.

## Verified current structure

Current Marzano Evaluation Center material identifies three Professional Responsibilities elements:

1. **Adhering to School and District Policies and Procedures**
2. **Maintaining Expertise in Content and Pedagogy**
3. **Promoting Teacher Leadership and Collaboration**

Current MEC material frames this domain as the professional foundation beneath effective teaching: teachers act as professionals, continue developing expertise, and contribute to leadership and shared responsibility beyond a single classroom.

## Dossier rule

Every dossier separates:

1. **Verified public-source claims**
2. **Teaching Practice Lab interpretation**
3. **Original examples and exercises**
4. **Unresolved protocol-level details**

Do not promote an interpretation into an official Marzano claim.

## Shared reasoning questions

For every element, Teaching Practice Lab should train the teacher to answer:

- What professional responsibility is actually being demonstrated?
- What evidence shows the responsibility is being carried out well?
- What surface behavior could look compliant while missing the purpose?
- Where does professional judgment end and a binding requirement begin?
- How does this responsibility affect students, colleagues, or the instructional system?
- What would improvement look like over time?
- How would I defend this decision to an administrator or colleague?
