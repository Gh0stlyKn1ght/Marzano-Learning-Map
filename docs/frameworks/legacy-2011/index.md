# Historical 2011 Model

The user-supplied **Marzano Art and Science of Teaching Teacher Evaluation Model Learning Map (2011)** documents the older framework with **60 elements across four domains**.

Domain 1 contains 41 classroom-strategy/behavior elements organized through nine Design Questions. The map notes that DQ10 continues into Domain 2. Domains 2 through 4 cover planning/preparing, reflecting on teaching, and collegiality/professionalism.

## Why retain it

- Understand the lineage of later Marzano models.
- Compare how older practices were consolidated or reframed.
- Recognize legacy terminology that may still appear in district documents or professional conversations.

## Why it is not our default training model

Current public Marzano Evaluation Center material presents the Focused Teacher Evaluation Model as the active teacher model with 23 competencies across four domains.

## Copyright warning

The supplied 2011 map carries an explicit digitization restriction. Do not commit the source PDF or recreate its full visual map/rubric in this public repository. Use high-level factual notes, original analysis, and lawful citations instead.

## Crosswalk status

No home-grown legacy-to-FTEM crosswalk should be treated as official. Any future crosswalk in this repository must be clearly labeled either:

- **Official**, with a cited authoritative source, or
