# Challenge System

The challenge system is the heart of the learning platform. It should progressively remove hints and require increasingly independent instructional reasoning.

## Challenge families

### Recall

Short-answer retrieval without notes.

Examples:

- Which domain contains this competency?
- What is the instructional purpose?
- What desired effect are you looking for?
- Give one example and one non-example.

### Recognition

Given a classroom situation, identify the most relevant competency and explain why.

### VS Mode

Compare easily confused concepts.

Examples:

- Previewing New Content vs Reviewing Content
- Processing New Content vs Elaborating on Content
- Engagement vs Organizing Students to Interact with Content
- Examining Reasoning vs Revising Knowledge

A correct answer must explain why the competing answer is less appropriate.

### Non-Example Lab

Determine whether a visible classroom behavior actually demonstrates the practice.

Example:

> Students are completing a Kahoot and appear excited.

The learner must decide what additional evidence is needed before concluding that an engagement practice produced the intended effect.

### Find the Error

Critique flawed instructional reasoning.

Example:

> "Students are debugging syntax errors, so they are examining their reasoning."

The learner must identify what is missing before that claim is defensible.

### Evidence Scanner

Given a scenario, choose which observations actually support the claimed desired effect. Distractors should include surface behaviors that look positive but are not sufficient evidence.

### Lesson Simulator

Given a topic, standard, learner context, and time limit, construct an instructional sequence and defend the selected practices.

### Reverse Identification

Start with authentic classroom evidence rather than a named competency. Determine what practice or combination of practices is actually occurring.

### Ambiguous Scenario

Some scenarios intentionally do not contain enough evidence. The correct response may be:

> Insufficient evidence to determine.

This must be a first-class answer state, not an error condition.

## Difficulty tiers

### Beginner

- competency named or strongly hinted,
- limited distractors,
- explanations available,
- one reasoning step.

### Practitioner

- no direct hint,
- nearby competencies used as distractors,
- evidence must be interpreted,
- multiple reasoning steps.

### Expert

- incomplete or ambiguous evidence,
- multiple practices may be present,
- learner must ask what additional evidence is needed,
- explanation and adaptation required,
- no answer-by-keyword shortcuts.

## Defense modes

### Principal

Questions focus on purpose, alignment, evidence, and instructional decision-making.

### Instructional Coach

Questions probe why a strategy worked or failed and what should change next.

### Skeptic

Challenges superficial language and asks whether the named practice is really different from ordinary classroom activity.

### Framework Expert

Requires precise distinctions among related competencies and framework terminology.

### Rapid Fire

Short response window. Domain, purpose, desired effect, evidence, adaptation, next move.

## Socratic response behavior

The coach should not reward vague responses.

Weak:

> "I used groups because group work increases engagement."

Expected feedback:

> The answer identifies a possible benefit but not the instructional mechanism. Explain what students were expected to do cognitively with the content and what evidence would show that it occurred.

The system should grade reasoning dimensions separately from tone or vocabulary.

## Boss fights

A domain boss is an integrated performance task rather than a long multiple-choice test.

A boss may require the learner to:

1. analyze a complete lesson,
2. identify relevant practices,
3. distinguish overlapping practices,
4. evaluate evidence,
5. diagnose a failed desired effect,
6. select an adaptation,
7. defend the decision,
8. transfer the reasoning to a second context.

Example result:

```text
DOMAIN BOSS: STANDARDS-BASED INSTRUCTION

Recognition       92%
Discrimination    88%
Evidence          73%
Diagnosis         89%
Adaptation        77%
Defense           81%
Transfer          74%

RESULT: CLEARED
Rank gate unlocked
```

Boss performance should influence rank and mastery confidence more strongly than routine XP.
