# Source Registry

This is the human-readable source registry for Teaching Practice Lab. It records the sources that are allowed to drive framework claims, course design decisions, and technical architecture.

A source appearing here does **not** mean its text, graphics, protocols, scales, or training materials may be republished. The registry records provenance, not redistribution rights.

## Source hierarchy

When sources disagree or details are incomplete:

1. current official MEC model/protocol material,
2. current official MEC articles or reports,
3. licensed district/state implementation materials when legally available,
4. peer-reviewed research for learning-science claims,
5. historical Marzano material for lineage/context,
6. secondary commentary only for discovery or comparison.

Historical or secondary material must never silently override the current FTEM model.

## Rules

- Add a source here before treating it as foundational.
- Prefer canonical publisher URLs.
- Distinguish framework claims from Teaching Practice Lab design decisions.
- Do not invent exact labels or protocol language.
- Do not republish restricted materials merely for agent convenience.
- A registry entry does not imply redistribution rights.
- Course examples, scenarios, diagrams, comparisons, and explanations should be original unless a source is quoted within copyright limits.
